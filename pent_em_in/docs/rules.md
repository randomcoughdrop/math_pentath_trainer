# Pent 'Em In™ — Gameplay Reference

Source: Pent 'Em In™ Tournament Highlights, Mathematics Pentathlon® (Division IV, Grades 6–7), Pentathlon Institute, Inc. Board layout confirmed by the user.

---

## Overview

Pent 'Em In is a two-player transformational geometry game played on a 13 × 9 grid. Each player uses 5 pentominoes and 2 blocking chips. Players place their pentominoes in a placement phase, then try to entrap all of the opponent's pentominoes by rotating, flipping, or sliding their own pieces in a movement phase.

---

## Goal (Ways to Win)

1. **Entrap:** Entrap all 5 of the opponent's pentominoes.
2. **Placement win:** The opponent cannot place all 5 pentominoes during placement.
3. **Repetition win:** You correctly challenge the opponent for moving the same pentomino three consecutive times, even with chip movement interspersed.
4. **Monitor award:** The Game Monitor awards a win if the opponent has no legal move.

**Ties:** If time is called and neither player has won, the Game Monitor declares a tie.

---

## Board

- **Size:** 13 columns × 9 rows.
- **Sheriff stars (B):** Four star squares, at column 3 and column 11 in rows 3 and 7. No pentomino may be placed on a star, and chips may not slide through or land on one.

### Starting Chip Positions (row, column; 1-indexed)

| Player | Chip | Position |
|--------|------|----------|
| Player 1 | Chip A | Row 4, Column 7 |
| Player 1 | Chip B | Row 5, Column 6 |
| Player 2 | Chip A | Row 5, Column 8 |
| Player 2 | Chip B | Row 6, Column 7 |

## Setup and Start

1. The Game Monitor places one chip of one color in each hand, out of view of the players.
2. One player chooses a hand. The player holding the starting color receives the **Movement Record Flag Card** and play begins.
3. The flag card passes to the opponent at the end of each turn.

---

## Phase I: Placement

- Each player places **exactly 5 pentominoes** on the gameboard. A sixth pentomino may not be placed.
- No pentomino may be placed over a **sheriff star**.
- A pentomino may **not share a common edge** with an opponent's pentomino.
- If a player picks up a piece and cannot place it, that player may select another piece.
- Once a piece has been placed, it **cannot be changed**.
- If a player cannot place a pentomino, that player **forfeits** the game.

---

## Phase II: Movement

### Pentomino Movement

- A pentomino may be **slid**, **flipped**, or **rotated**.
- **No combination** of these movements may be made within a single turn.
- A pentomino may be **flipped or rotated over** chips, sheriff stars, or parts of another pentomino.
- A pentomino may **not be slid over** spaces occupied by chips or pentominoes, or over sheriff stars.
- Pentominoes may be moved so that they **share a common edge** with the opponent's pentominoes.
- Playing pieces may move into or through unoccupied **boot and hat** spaces.

### Chip Movement

- Chips may be moved after the placement phase.
- A chip may **not be slid through or placed on** a sheriff star.
- A player may **not make two consecutive chip movements**. If a chip is moved during one turn, the player must move a pentomino on the next turn.

### Movement Restrictions

- **Three-in-a-row rule:** A player may **not move the same pentomino three consecutive times**, even if interspersed with chip movement. Being challenged for this results in a **loss of the game**.
- **Commitment:** Once a player places or moves a piece and releases hold of it, that piece **may not be repositioned**.
- **Completing a turn:** If a player has moved a piece off its space, that piece must be moved to complete the turn.

### Tracking Movement

- During Phase II, players **must track every move** on the **Movement Record Flag Card**.

---

## Turn Structure

- **Time limit:** Each turn must be completed within **one minute**. If a player exceeds this, the Monitor reminds the player. If the player still does not complete the turn, the player **forfeits the turn** in all games except Pent 'Em In.
  - *Note:* Pent 'Em In is the exception, so this forfeit does not apply to this game.
- **Flag card:** A player must be in possession of the flag card **while taking a turn**. The player does not have to hold the card while completing the turn.
- **Declaring a win:** A player must announce a win **while in possession of the flag card**.
- **Time called:** If time is called and the beginning player has touched a piece, both players are allowed to complete a turn.

---

## Challenges

### When to Challenge

A challenge must be made **upon receiving the flag card and before touching a pentomino or chip**. Challenges may not be issued on the starting rules, the passing of the flag card, or exceeding the one-minute time limit.

### Challenge Outcomes

| Challenge Type | Correct (C) | Incorrect (I) / Inappropriate (IA) |
|----------------|-------------|------------------------------------|
| **Initial placement** | Game Monitor removes the placed piece. Challenger selects and places an opponent's piece correctly. | Challenged player may move one of the opponent's chips to a new location. Challenger begins a turn. Record "I" (incorrect). |
| **Other challenges** | Game Monitor restores the board to its prior status. Challenger either begins a turn or forces the opponent to move a pentomino. Record "C." | Challenger loses the turn, or is forced to move by the challenged player. Record "I." |
| **Three-in-a-row move** | Game Monitor declares the win to the challenger. | — |
| **Declared win** | — | A declared win may be challenged even without holding the flag card. The Monitor asks the player to state the nature of the challenge. If correct, the challenger wins. If incorrect, the declared win stands. |

### Inappropriate Challenges

An **inappropriate challenge (IA)** is one made at the wrong time, or about a procedure that is not a tournament rule. An IA does **not** cost the challenger a turn, but it is recorded.

### Penalties

- **Three "I"s and/or "IA"s** in any combination result in an **automatic loss**, declared by the Monitor. The Monitor may warn players after their first and second "I" or "IA."

### Challenge Procedure

- If a challenge involves more than one rule, each challenged rule is treated as a separate challenge, ruled on in the order the player announced them.
- A player may request a **2nd opinion** from the Game Leader, and then a **3rd and final opinion** from the Tournament Director, by holding up the Opinion Card.
- Once play has resumed, earlier rulings will **not** be reconsidered.

---

## Tournament Conduct

- Players must treat adults and fellow Pentathletes with courtesy and respect. Infractions, including intimidation or uncooperative behavior, may result in 0 points or removal from the tournament site.
- **Scratch paper** is provided for all games.
- The **Score Card** is the only official record of a score. Players verify and sign their own score cards.
- **Do not clear** the gameboard, pieces, or playing area until score cards are collected. Leave everything intact after a win is declared.
- Players remain **seated** until score cards are checked and picked up by a Game Leader.

## Quick-Reference Checklist

**Placement**
- [ ] Exactly 5 pieces
- [ ] Not on a star
- [ ] Not sharing an edge with opponent's piece
- [ ] Placed pieces are final

**Movement**
- [ ] One action per turn: slide, flip, OR rotate (not combined)
- [ ] No sliding over chips, pieces, or stars
- [ ] Flipping/rotating over chips, stars, or pieces is allowed
- [ ] No more than two chip moves in a row
- [ ] Same piece not moved three turns in a row
- [ ] Record every move on the Movement Record Flag Card
- [ ] Announce a win while holding the flag card
